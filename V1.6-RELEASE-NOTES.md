# Food Passport v1.6 — Food Explorer

## What changed
- Removed the large empty district-selection panel; map uses full width until a district is selected.
- Selected district gets a much larger 45% desktop Food Explorer.
- Food cards are a 2-column browsing grid with category accent colours.
- On mobile, selecting a district opens an 86vh bottom sheet for easy food browsing.
- Bangladesh map now uses 8 division colour families and all 64 district names are drawn on the SVG map.
- Map colour gets richer as more foods are tasted; completed districts get a gold outline.
- Report redesigned with green/blue/red accent cards and a colourful progress map.
- Added Edit Passport modal, Reset Tasted, Clear Wishlist, and Reset Everything.
- Existing food cards remain individually editable through the ✓ toggle.
- Database remains at 330 food experiences; V1.6 focuses on browsing quality and presentation.
